SmartRoute-Bench base suite (English) — run base-v1. 57 English real-work tasks across 12 categories, run identically for three pinned frontier models and one smart-routing virtual model. The routing arm used an unmodified cost-quality-balanced policy over a 4-model pool. Costs are gateway-side metered sale prices; the KRW figures in the tables convert at the gateway billing rate on the measurement date (₩1,504/USD).

ResultLowest total cost, 32/32 objective checks — the routing arm and GPT-5.6 Sol are the only two at a perfect check score

  • Overall (57 tasks) — smart routing had the lowest total cost (₩4,622 / ~$3.07), 76% of GPT-5.6 Sol, 61% of Opus, 34% of Fable 5, while scoring a chat judge mean of 9.19 (97.6% of Sol's 9.417) and passing 32/32 objective checks, matched only by Sol (Opus 31/32, Fable 5 23/32).
  • Structured tasks at frontier parity — on extraction, JSON generation, and reasoning the routed low-cost model scored 10.0, level with the pinned frontier models. Deterministic-answer workloads route to cheap models with no measurable quality loss.
  • Where the gap remains — summarization (9.54 vs 9.81) and translation (9.53 vs 9.64) trail the best pinned arm by a few tenths. Long-form generation is where a capability-profile-aware policy has the most to gain.
  • Delivery — Fable 5 had 8 undelivered responses (safety-classifier false-positive refusals, listed in the delivery-failure table). Excluding undelivered, Fable 5's chat judge is 9.51 — the shortfall is delivery reliability, not capability.
  • Routing distribution — every one of the 59 chat routing decisions selected google/gemini-3.5-flash: with the balanced strategy the pool's cheapest, fastest model wins across categories. This is a property of the policy under test, not of the benchmark.

Judge panelThree independent providers, blind, self-bias audited

  • Three judge models from different providers (Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.6 Sol, Gemini 3.1 Pro) score each response blind; the mean is the headline. The per-judge × per-arm table below exposes any same-vendor preference, and pairwise agreement is reported alongside.
  • Objective categories (coding, extraction, JSON generation, reasoning, agentic) do not depend on the panel at all — they pass or fail deterministic checks run in a sandbox.
